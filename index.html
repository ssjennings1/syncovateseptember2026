Your team keeps coming to you for everything. You've tried fixing it. It came back.

That's not a people problem. It's a structural one. The way decisions get made, ownership gets defined, and authority flows — all of it organized itself around you. Not by design. That's just the nature of growth.

The issue isn't visible from inside what you built.

It's that smart people — in trades, manufacturing, family businesses — build systems around themselves without realizing it. The processes, the communication patterns, the culture: all of it made sense when you built it. Then the business grew, and what held it together started working against it.

I find the gap between where you think you are and where you actually are. Then we figure out the next best step to get moving in the right direction.

You feel it before you can name it.

  • Good people underperforming.
  • Meetings that produce motion but not movement.
  • A leadership team that agrees in the room and does their own thing once they leave.
  • Someone you love in a role they can't grow into, and everyone can feel it.
In twenty years of this work, I've never walked into a leadership system where the blind spot was created by bad intentions. It's almost always good intentions — and the assumptions that grew up quietly underneath them.

Not sure which one? That's what the first conversation is for.

The higher you climb, the fewer people tell you the truth.

Your team watches what you react to. Your partners — or your family — want certainty. Your peers are competitors. There's no one left who will tell you the thing you actually need to hear — or ask the question that cracks the problem open.

That's the gap I work in. I bring the analytical tools of a strategist and the human insight of a clinician — 20 years inside leadership systems, trained in the psychology of organizations and the architecture of how they actually behave under pressure. I see what the dynamics in your business are doing to your results. And I know how to work with people through the hard parts, not just around them.

Sometimes that's a single session to get clarity on a decision that's been sitting too long. Sometimes it's a year of calls that become the one room where you don't have to be the one with the answers. You don't have to filter with me.
