Programs built for engagement, tailored to your room.

Available as a 45–60 minute keynote, a 90-minute interactive working session, or an expanded half-day or full-day retreat framework.

  • Your Go-To Power Move (And Why It's Not Enough Anymore)

    The Anatomy of Leadership Influence

    Every leader has a default tool they reach for when the pressure spikes. Some lean on authority; some lean on technical expertise; others rely on relationships or personal charisma. But what built your initial success often becomes the very bottleneck that stalls your organization. Grounded in the classic French & Raven bases of social power, this session leads participants through a tactical self-audit to identify their default power moves, surface their blind spots under stress, and learn how to shift their teams from mere compliance to genuine ownership.

    Best for: Annual conferences, leadership summits, CEO peer forums, rising-executive academies.

    Key takeaway: A personal profile of how you actually exert influence under pressure, and how to diversify your power base so your team stops bottlenecking at your desk.

  • Keeping It Human: Friction, Trust, and Connection in an Automated World

    The Neuroscience of High-Performing Teams

    When technology and AI eliminate operational friction, the remaining friction is entirely human. Many organizations mistake speed for effectiveness and polite agreement for alignment. In this session, Dr. J examines why healthy teams actually require friction, how leaders accidentally create "polite avoidance," and how to cultivate the psychological safety that allows hard truths to be spoken out loud without blowing up relationships.

    Best for: Executive retreats, cross-functional leadership gatherings, industry associations navigating rapid technological change.

    Key takeaway: The three observable signals of fake consensus, and a practical cadence for turning tension into clarity.

  • The Architecture of the Blind Spot

    Why Smart Companies Build Systems That Work Against Them

    In twenty years of organizational work, the biggest breakdowns rarely stem from bad intentions. They stem from good intentions that created quiet, unintended structures underneath them. As companies grow through the $2M to $40M corridor, the communication loops, decision pathways, and authority structures that once enabled survival begin to suffocate scalability. Dr. J breaks down how to see what your daily proximity hides from you.

    Best for: Entrepreneurial networks, privately held company summits, family enterprise conferences.

    Key takeaway: A diagnostic framework to identify whether a recurring breakdown is an individual performance issue or a structural architecture problem.
